Privacy Policy for Fox Off

Effective Date: November 2026 · Version 1.0

🛡️ Zero-Trust Architecture Guarantee

Fox Off (For Fox Sake) is engineered from the ground up with an absolute zero-trust privacy-by-design policy. We believe content purifiers should never become content eavesdroppers.

1. No Data Collection or Transmission

We do not collect, store, transmit, log, or sell any of the following:

  • Personal data or device identifiers.
  • Browsing history, requested URLs, or page contents.
  • Telemetry, analytics, or behavioral cookies.
  • IP addresses or user interaction logs.

2. 100% Client-Side Local Execution

All webpage sanitization, tracker defusing, ad removal, and typography reformatting occur strictly on your device inside an isolated, read-only client-side sandbox.

No webpage HTML or input text is sent to our servers. Any external fetch requests you make to purify a URL run directly from your browser or client runtime.

3. Zero Accounts & Zero Tracking

Fox Off requires no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. The app operates completely standalone as a Progressive Web App (PWA) and mobile hybrid application.

4. Local Storage Only

If you choose to use the "Save for Offline" feature or customize your reader preferences (such as font size, family, or color theme), those preferences are saved exclusively within your local browser storage (localStorage) and never leave your device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at:

support@foxoff.app or open an issue on GitHub.
